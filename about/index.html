Work

I am an Art Director and Graphic Designer, currently working and living in Barcelona. My projects focus on both digital and print design. I hold the position of Lead Graphic Designer & Art Director at Planeta Publishing House, where, while designing books I have the opportunity to learn a bunch of things that I didn't even know I was interested in.

In addition to my full-time commitment, I also work as a lecturer at LCI Barcelona and Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC), where I teach graphic design students about editorial design.
